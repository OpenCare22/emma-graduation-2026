2016
Age 8 · After school

Joining the football team

Number eleven

She was the only girl on the under-tens for one full season and politely refused to mention it. Played left wing. Scored twice. Cried once when the team lost the final, then ate three pieces of cake at the after-party.

2018
Age 10 · A new instrument

The piano year

photo · upright, sunlight, half a smile

A second-hand upright arrived in May and she taught it to behave. By December she could play "Clair de Lune" with both hands, mostly in the right order, and would do so on request, then on demand.

2020
Age 12 · The year inside

A strange spring

March – June · home

Lessons over a kitchen table

Took up bread, gave up bread, took up bread again. Learned French verbs from a duck-themed app. Held a virtual birthday party with seventeen guests in nine countries and three time zones.

"I think I prefer real school, actually."

2022
Age 14 · Beginning of high school

A new uniform

photo · gymnasium gates, first morning
Gymnázium · September

First day, second act

She walked in twice as tall as the last time we'd dropped her at a gate. Made three friends by the second week and a fourth one by accident. Decided she liked history more than she'd planned to.

2024
Age 16 · The world widens

An exchange year

photo · Lisbon balcony, late light

Six months in Lisbon with a host family who taught her two new languages — Portuguese, and how to order coffee with confidence. Came back with a notebook full of bus tickets and a small, unmistakable gravity.

2026
Age 18 · Now

And here we are

Eighteen years of small mornings and big nights, of sandwiches packed and concerts attended, of report cards and sprained ankles and library cards renewed. All of it, somehow, pointing here.
