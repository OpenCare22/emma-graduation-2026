The soft list

"I want to write something good enough that I'd reread it when I'm forty."

— Emma · in a notebook · February 2026

  1. 01
    Publish a small thing somewhere small. A poem, an essay, anything with a real ISSN.
  2. 02
    Become genuinely good at one instrument. Piano leads the polling; cello has been rising in the months.
  3. 03
    Read every book on Grandfather's shelf. Forty-seven books. Two are in Latin. We will see.
  4. 04
    Live, briefly, in a city without speaking the language. Tbilisi has been mentioned. So has Hanoi.
  5. 05
    Be the kind of friend her friends call first. She is, mostly, already doing this one.
Fin
